Project overview

This report applies data visualisation to the Sample Superstore dataset (Kaggle, 2023) — 9,994 US retail orders from 2014–2017 — to identify where a mid-sized retailer is winning and losing money. The build moves through five stages: collecting the data, preparing it in Power Query, designing seven visuals and two slicers in Power BI, analysing what they show, and turning that analysis into stakeholder-ready decisions.

Findings

Five things the data makes clear

Technology carries the business

Highest-profit segment, while Furniture's low returns point to a pricing problem.

Q4 is the season that matters

Sales and profit peak reliably in the final quarter.

A regional performance gap

West leads on every measure; Central trails on all of them.

Discounting past 20% loses money

A consistent driver of loss, not a marginal effect.

Consumers are the priority segment

Nearly half of all profit comes from Consumer, making retention the highest-leverage lever.

Business evidence-based decisions

What the dashboard supports doing next

—

Prioritise Technology and re-examine Furniture's pricing and cost structure.

—

Plan and fund Q4 marketing and stock capacity ahead of the October–December peak.

—

Apply the West region's approach to lift performance in the Central region.

—

Cap discounts at 20% to stop orders tipping into a loss.

—

Invest in Consumer-segment loyalty and retention, given its share of total profit.
